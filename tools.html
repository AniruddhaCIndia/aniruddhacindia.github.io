Gravitational Lensing

A massive foreground object bends the path of light behind it, distorting the image of anything beyond it — the same way a lens in your glasses does. Click and drag inside the left panel to move the background source, and watch how it warps into the lensed image on the right.

Left: source. Right: lensed image, distorted by a singular isothermal ellipsoid (SIE) mass profile.
