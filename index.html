About

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics at TIFR, Mumbai, working on the lensing of gravitational waves — how intervening massive objects bend and distort GW signals, and what that distortion can teach us about the universe. My research focuses on developing model-independent techniques to detect lensed gravitational-wave signals and exploring their applications, from precision tests of general relativity to probing cosmology.

Research Interests

  • Gravitational lensing of gravitational waves
  • Model-independent detection of lensed GW signals
  • Applications: tests of general relativity and cosmology
